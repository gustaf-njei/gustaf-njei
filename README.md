# Gustaf Njei

Electrical and energy systems engineer (B.S., Northeastern University) building AI and energy software. Based in Douala, Cameroon.

I build tools that turn energy data into decisions: solar monitoring, microgrid sizing, and AI pipelines that convert raw telemetry into clear status reports. My foundation is in smart-grid research (power system simulation in MATLAB) and hardware engineering (instrument-board testing and debugging, C++ in Simulink). I've been building independently since September 2025.
